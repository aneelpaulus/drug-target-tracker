Stage labels. FDA-approved: an FDA approval or license record exists (earliest approval date shown when available). Approved outside US: approved by another regulator, with no FDA record found. Clinical / investigational: tested in people but not approved anywhere. Preclinical: lab or animal work only. Labels come from Open Targets (which draws on ChEMBL), FDA records via openFDA, and ClinicalTrials.gov, and are automatic, so confirm anything important.

Sources. Drugs: Open Targets Platform, openFDA (Drugs@FDA), ClinicalTrials.gov. Literature: PubMed and preprints (bioRxiv, medRxiv, and others) via Europe PMC. Meeting abstracts via Crossref: ASCO (Journal of Clinical Oncology supplements), ASH (Blood supplements), AACR (Cancer Research supplements), AACR-NCI-EORTC (Molecular Cancer Therapeutics supplements), ESMO (Annals of Oncology supplements), and EHA (HemaSphere supplements through 2023). Abstracts are matched on title only.
